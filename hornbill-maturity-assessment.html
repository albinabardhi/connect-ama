Use these descriptions to calibrate your scores. The "what you'll hear" phrases are the fastest way to recognise which stage you're actually at — not where you'd like to be.

Stage 1
Undefined
Processes lack structure and documentation. Outcomes depend entirely on individual knowledge and experience. Two agents handle the same request differently. There is no consistent categorisation, no enforced SLAs, and no meaningful reporting.
What you'll hear"It depends on who's on."
What you'll seeNo queue visibility. Tickets arrive by email or phone.
RiskKey knowledge walks out the door when agents leave.
Stage 2
Defined
Processes are documented but not digitalised. Procedures exist in SharePoint, Confluence, or a wiki. Teams know about them but don't consistently follow them. The system doesn't enforce the process — discipline does.
What you'll hear"We have a document for that, but nobody really follows it."
What you'll seeSLAs exist on paper. Approvals happen via email.
RiskDocumentation goes stale fast. Compliance audits are stressful.
Stage 3
Digital workflow
Processes run inside Hornbill as structured BPM workflows. Agents are guided step by step. Outcomes are consistent regardless of who handles the ticket. SLAs are tracked by the system. Reporting becomes meaningful for the first time. Most mature Hornbill customers are here.
What you'll hear"Every ticket goes through the same workflow in Hornbill."
What you'll seeReal-time queue dashboards. SLA breach alerts from the system.
FoundationThis is the prerequisite for all automation above.
Stage 4
Partial automation
Automated steps run alongside human decision-making. Auto-assignment, notifications, and third-party integrations (AD, SCCM, Intune) handle routine mechanics. Agents focus on diagnosis and decisions. The balance between automated and human steps is actively managed.
What you'll hear"Auto-assignment and notifications run automatically."
What you'll seeIntegrations active. Agents handle exceptions, not admin.
Watch outDon't add automation before Stage 3 is stable.
Stage 5
End-to-end
Processes run from initiation to resolution with minimal human involvement. High-volume, low-risk, well-understood processes (password resets, standard access requests) execute fully automatically. Human intervention is only required for genuine exceptions.
What you'll hear"That request type runs without anyone touching it."
What you'll seeZero-touch fulfilment for defined request categories.
NoteNot every process should reach Stage 5 — nor needs to.
