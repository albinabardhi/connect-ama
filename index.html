Use these to calibrate your scores. The "what you'll hear" phrases are the fastest signal for each stage.

Stage 1
Undefined
No structure or documentation. Outcomes depend entirely on individual knowledge. Agents handle the same request differently every time.
What you'll hear"It depends on who's on."
What you'll seeNo queue visibility. Requests via email/phone only.
RiskKnowledge walks out the door when people leave.
Stage 2
Defined
Processes documented but not digitalised. Procedures exist in SharePoint or Confluence. Teams know about them but don't consistently follow them.
What you'll hear"We have a document, but nobody really follows it."
What you'll seeSLAs on paper. Approvals via email. Stale wikis.
RiskCompliance audits are stressful. Data lives in spreadsheets.
Stage 3
Digital workflow
Process runs as a structured BPM workflow in Hornbill. Agents guided step by step. SLAs tracked by the system. Reporting is meaningful. Most mature Hornbill customers are here.
What you'll hear"Every ticket goes through the same Hornbill workflow."
What you'll seeReal-time dashboards. SLA breach alerts. Consistent outcomes.
FoundationRequired before any automation above can be added.
Stage 4
Partial automation
Automated steps alongside human decisions. Auto-assignment, notifications, and integrations (AD, SCCM, Intune) handle routine work. Humans handle decisions and exceptions.
What you'll hear"Auto-assignment and notifications run automatically."
What you'll seeIntegrations active. Agents focus on judgment, not admin.
Watch outDon't add automation before Stage 3 is stable.
Stage 5
End-to-end
Process runs start to finish with minimal human involvement. High-volume, low-risk processes (password resets, standard access) execute fully automatically.
What you'll hear"That request type runs without anyone touching it."
What you'll seeZero-touch fulfilment for defined categories.
NoteNot every process should reach Stage 5.
